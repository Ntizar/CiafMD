# SAFETY RECOMMENDATIONS

| Addressee | Final Implementer | Number | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| AESF (NSA-ES) | AESF (NSA-ES), IM and RUs | 41/2025-1 | Ensure, through internal monitoring and supervision of communications between driving staff and signalling staff, strict compliance in all operational situations with the communication protocols established in the Safety Management Systems of the Infrastructure Manager and railway undertakings. |
| AESF (NSA-ES) | IM and RUs | 41/2025-2 | Reinforce training content for driving and signalling staff regarding the identification of risk situations in cases of trailing through points and/or setting back movements over track equipment with a risk of derailment. |
| AESF (NSA-ES) | ADIF and ADIF AV (IM) | 41/2025-3 | Analyse the operational characteristics of the “Train–Ground” system at Control Centres during the processes of trains entering and leaving the control band and, where appropriate, adopt measures to prevent unintended effects on train selections made by Signallers. |
| AESF (NSA-ES) | ADIF and ADIF AV (IM) | 41/2025-4 | Improve coordination and communications between Signallers managing adjacent control bands, whether within the same Control Centre or across different Control Centres. |
