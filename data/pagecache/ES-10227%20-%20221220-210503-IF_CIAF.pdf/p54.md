5. The derailment was caused by the uncontrolled movement of the standard gauge switch rail on the obtuse frog at the D2 turnout due to the passage of trains during the period of time between the 2021 works and the day of the derailment. Causing the switch rail to invade the free passage gauge of the wheel flanges on the right side of the train. This movement of the standard gauge switch rail caused the right-wheel flange of the wheelset (situated between the penultimate and last passenger car on the 4271 ALVIA train) to collide with the switch rail toe. The right wheel was guided by the standard gauge rail. The opposite wheel of that wheelset went up the common rail, being derailed. The next wheelset, situated entirely on the last passenger car, followed the same path. On the other hand, the two rear drive wheelsets were not able to climb the common rail, remaining on track.
