# MEASURES TAKEN SINCE THE EVENT

To the date of this report, AESF (Spanish NSA) has issued the application guidance of the Railway Driving Regulation F-RCF-01/2023, regarding “Special driving conditions”.

## ADDITIONAL REMARKS

The investigation has found (from the recordings of train #55203) that the braking regime of the locomotive was “type P”. The braking regime of the locomotive should have been “type G”, the same regime that the rest of the train had, as established by the documentation of the train.
