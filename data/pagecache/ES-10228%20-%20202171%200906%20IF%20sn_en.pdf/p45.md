4. The Instructions of Prat de Llobregat interlocking does not contemplate the incompatibilities between the automatic systems, such as the one mentioned in the previous paragraph, and the signallers’ commands, so it does not indicate that the operator must disconnect the automatic systems before executing certain orders. **Recommendation 71/2021-3.**

## Systemic factors

1. There is a fairly common practice when solving problems related to detection loss of the position of switches, consisting in acting quickly and without formalizing the intervention by the SIM team, to only position the switch in a specific position. After that, during the scheduled activities of preventive maintenance (usually overnight), the problem of detection loss of the position of switches is solved. **Recommendation 71/2021-1.**

2. The terminology used to specify the position of switch #4 by SIM team, which is generalized, was confusing. There is no procedure to unequivocally name the status and position of trackside apparatus in the communications between the SIM teams and the signallers, during tests, verifications and corrective maintenance actions. **Recommendation 71/2021-2.**
